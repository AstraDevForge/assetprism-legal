Support

Get help with AssetPrism.

Email astradev123@gmail.com with your app version, iOS version, and a short description of the issue. Redact sensitive financial information from screenshots. Never send bank credentials, passwords, account numbers, or card details.

Build your first plan

On first launch, choose Start my plan and enter your savings, income, spending, and chosen retirement age. The result opens when the plan is created. You can reopen it from View plan on the Dashboard or from the Plans tab.

Understand a projection

Baseline, Optimistic, and Pessimistic are simulated outcomes, not guaranteed future values. Tap the chart to inspect a value, or swipe vertically to see more of the plan. Use Edit plan to change inputs and Model assumptions to review the assumptions.

Restore Pro

Open the Pro purchase screen and choose Restore Purchases while signed in to the Apple Account that bought Pro. A subscription must be active; a Lifetime purchase can be restored if it has not been revoked or refunded.

Manage a subscription or refund

Apple handles billing, cancellation, and refund requests. See cancel a subscription or request a refund. Deleting AssetPrism does not cancel a subscription.

Local data

Plans and asset records are saved on your device, with no developer-operated cloud sync. We cannot retrieve lost local records. Manage any device backups separately; do not uninstall the app to troubleshoot without considering your data.
