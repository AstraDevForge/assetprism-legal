Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 2, 2026. This policy describes AssetPrism 2.0.0 and this support website.

Planning data stays on your device

Your manually entered assets, liabilities, income, spending, ages, life events, goals, forecasts, and preferences are processed and stored locally by the app. AssetPrism does not connect to your bank or require an account. The developer does not receive your financial inputs or simulation results through the app.

AssetPrism does not provide its own cloud synchronization or online backup. Device backups managed by Apple or your computer may include app data, depending on your settings. We cannot access those backups.

Analytics and advertising

AssetPrism 2.0.0 has no third-party analytics SDK, custom telemetry service, advertising SDK, or cross-app tracking. The developer does not collect app interaction events through an analytics service. Apple may provide App Store sales, download, and diagnostic information according to its own services and your device settings; this is separate from a developer-operated analytics SDK.

Purchases

Optional Pro purchases use Apple's StoreKit and the App Store. Apple processes payment and manages subscriptions. The app reads verified purchase and entitlement information to unlock Pro and caches the Lifetime entitlement on your device. We do not receive your payment card details or send purchase transactions to a developer-operated server. Apple's processing is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Support emails

If you email astradev123@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request and resolve the issue, and retain them only as needed for support or applicable legal obligations. Please remove account numbers and sensitive financial information from screenshots. Do not send bank credentials, passwords, or payment card information.

This website

These static pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. We add no analytics scripts, advertising, cookies, contact forms, or financial calculators to this website. GitHub may process request information, including IP addresses and browser information, to provide hosting and security. Its processing is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Opening an external link is subject to that provider's policy.

Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information you voluntarily send for support may be processed by our email provider, or disclosed when required by applicable law. Your planning data is not uploaded to us for sale or sharing.

Your choices and data deletion

You can remove records using the available in-app controls. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from the device; copies in device backups may remain until you manage those backups separately. Offloading an app may preserve its data. We cannot recover or delete your locally stored plans remotely. To request access to, correction of, or deletion of information sent in a support email, contact us at the address above. Rights available to you depend on applicable law.

Children

AssetPrism is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support. Contact us if a child has sent personal information so we can address it.

Changes and contact

If the app's data practices change, we will update this policy and its effective date before the new practices take effect. Questions can be sent to the developer of AssetPrism at astradev123@gmail.com.
